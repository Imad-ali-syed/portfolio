What this club is

The CCNY Cybersecurity Club exists so students at City College have somewhere to actually practise security instead of only reading about it. We run competitions, build things, break things we're allowed to break, and bring in people who do this for a living.

You do not need a security background. You do not need to be a computer science major. A good chunk of the club started at zero, and the first workshop of every series assumes exactly that. If you're curious enough to show up, you belong here.

Formally, we're a registered student organization under the Graduate Student Council. Graduate students are the voting members; undergraduates, alumni, faculty and staff are all welcome as associate members and at every single event.

What we run

Hackathons

Weekend builds where you ship something real and walk away with a project worth putting on a résumé.

CTF competitions

Capture-the-flag: security puzzles solved in teams. We practise together and enter regional and national events.

Hands-on workshops

Network defense, penetration testing, digital forensics, cryptography. Laptops open, no slide decks for an hour.

Speaker events

Talks and panels with industry practitioners, academic researchers and government security professionals.

Certification study groups

Security+, CySA+, OSCP and CEH. Study with people on the same timeline instead of grinding alone.

Research & career prep

Faculty-mentored research projects, plus résumé reviews and mock interviews before internship season.

How to join

  1. 01 Fill out the signup form with your CCNY student email and your EMPLID.
  2. 02 We verify you're a current CCNY student. Usually same week.
  3. 03 Once you're verified your name goes up on the member wall below, and you're in.
  4. 04 Join the Discord and come to a general meeting: Thursdays, 12:00–2:00 PM, NAC Plaza & Quad.

There's no application to write and no membership fee. Everyone starts as a Member; leads and officer roles get handed out as people take things on.
