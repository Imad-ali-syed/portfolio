MAC 246 deadlines

Advanced Network Security. Every Saturday of the semester, what we cover, and what you submit.

SaturdaysRoom B-332Instructor: Imad Ali Syed

Labs are individual

There are five: the setup lab and Labs 1 to 4. Each of Labs 1 to 4 runs over two Saturdays, and your own report is due the Saturday after.

The project is group work

Pick one project with your team. If you finish a lab early, use the second lab session to work on it together.

No midterm or final exam

Your grade comes from labs, homework, the project and showing up. Presentations are on December 12.

Late work and questions

Late work is accepted only with a serious reason and the instructor's approval. Approved late work loses 20% for every three days and is not accepted after one week. Online submissions close automatically at the deadline.

How and where to submit each item is announced in class.
