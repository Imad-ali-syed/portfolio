About Me

I'm Imad Ali Syed, a cybersecurity graduate student at the City College of New York. I focus on security operations, threat detection and network traffic analysis, and I've built AI-driven intrusion detection and phishing detection systems in Python.

I also teach. I'm an adjunct lecturer at LaGuardia Community College and at City College, where I get to turn attack simulations and packet captures into hands-on labs for students.

Outside class I lead the CCNY Cybersecurity Club, compete in cyber competitions, and keep building web tools, including this site.
